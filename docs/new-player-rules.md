# Other Gods: New Player Rules

This guide is for your first few games. If a card directly contradicts this guide, the card text wins.

## Goal

Reduce your opponent's followers from `100` to `0`.

## What Starts in Play

- Each player starts with `100` followers and `0` mana.
- Your `God` starts in the god slot.
- Up to `3` `Powers` start in your power slots, face-down and locked.
- Shuffle the rest of your deck and draw `5` cards.
- A random player goes first. The second player starts with `+2` mana.

## The Board

- You have `5` front line spaces and `5` reserve line spaces.
- Front line creatures are your main attackers.
- Reserve creatures help defend, intercept, and support your front line.
- Your other zones are your hand, deck, graveyard, abyss, god slot, and power slots.

## Reading a Card

Not every card costs only mana.

- `3M` = pay `3` mana.
- `1D` = discard `1` card from your hand.
- `1S` = sacrifice `1` creature you control.

Some cards can also ask you to banish or shelve cards as part of the cost. You must pay every listed cost before the card can be played or activated.

Other common card info:

- `STR` = strength, used for attacking and many fights.
- `RES` = resilience, used for holding the line and surviving certain combats.
- `SPD` = speed, which matters for responses and interceptions.

## Card Types

- `God`: your main deity in play from the start. Gods usually provide a passive effect or activatable ability.
- `Power`: starts in play face-down and locked. You unlock it by paying its listed cost.
- `Creature`: a unit you place on the board to attack, defend, and intercept.
- `Structure`: a permanent board card that usually protects or supports.
- `Equipment`: attaches to a creature and changes its stats or abilities.
- `Spell`: a one-shot effect that usually resolves and then goes to the graveyard.
- `Hex`: usually prepared face-down before it can trigger.
- `Charm`: a fast trick or answer, sometimes played from hand and sometimes prepared.

## Turn Flow

At the start of your turn:

1. You gain `+1` mana.
2. You choose one upkeep option: `Draw Card` or `Gain 4 Mana`.

After that, you can:

- play cards
- unlock or use powers
- move creatures
- change creature stance
- attack
- use effects

At end of turn, play passes to your opponent. If you are above `7` cards in hand, you must discard down to `7`.

## Playing Cards

- Most cards are played from your hand on your own turn.
- Some cards can be played as fast responses if their speed and timing allow it.
- Some cards must be prepared face-down on your board first.
- Powers are different: they begin in play but locked, and you pay their listed cost to unlock them.

## Creatures, Stances, and Attacking

- Only creatures in the `front line` can declare attacks.
- Creatures must be in `Aggressive` stance to attack.
- `Aggressive` creatures fight with `STR`.
- `Defensive` creatures are better at holding ground and intercepting.
- Newly played creatures cannot move or change stance until your next turn.
- No attacks can be declared on the very first turn of the game.

## Movement and Intercepts

- Moving and changing stance use a creature's actions for the turn.
- Creatures can usually move only to nearby friendly spaces.
- When an attack is declared, another creature may be able to intercept and become the new defender.
- Defensive creatures intercept more easily.
- `Reach` increases how far a creature can intercept.

## Combat Basics

- If an attack hits the enemy followers directly, that player loses followers equal to the attacker's `STR`.
- Against an `Aggressive` creature, both sides fight with `STR`.
- Against a `Defensive` creature, the attacker compares `STR` to the defender's `RES`.
- Structures are usually beaten by dealing enough `STR` to overcome their `RES`.
- Attacking the enemy god slot is effectively attacking that player's followers.

## Powers and Gods

- Your God is active from the start of the game.
- Powers begin locked and face-down.
- Unlocking a power uses the cost printed on it, which may be mana, discard, sacrifice, or a mix.
- Once unlocked, a power can provide passive benefits or activated abilities.

## Deckbuilding Basics

- Exactly `1` God.
- Up to `3` Powers.
- At least `35` regular cards in the main deck.
- Most regular cards are limited to `3` copies.
- Legendary cards are limited to `1` copy each.
- Power cards must match your God's culture unless the power is `Neutral`.

## Good First-Game Tips

- Put creatures in the front line if you want to pressure your opponent.
- Keep defenders back when you need to protect followers.
- Watch card costs carefully: some strong cards ask for discards or sacrifices instead of extra mana.
- Unlocking the right power at the right time can swing the game.
- If you are unsure, start by choosing `Draw Card` during upkeep until you learn your deck's rhythm.
